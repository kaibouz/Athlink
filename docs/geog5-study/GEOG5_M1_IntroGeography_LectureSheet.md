## 1. Definitions（暗記）

| | English | 日本語メモ |
|---|---|---|
| Classic | Geography = **the study of the Earth as the home of humans** | 地球を「人間のすみか」として見る学問 |
| Detailed | A science that studies **relationships** among natural systems, geographic areas, society, cultural activities, and their **interdependence over space** | 「なに」より「空間上のつながり」 |

焦点は深海・宇宙ではなく、人間が関わる地表付近。学期で扱う **4 spheres:**

1. **Atmosphere**（大気）
2. **Hydrosphere**（水圏）
3. **Biosphere**（生物圏）
4. **Lithosphere**（岩石圏／地盤）

## 2. Realms of geography（図を頭に描く）

```
        Physical geography          Human geography
        (natural sciences           (social sciences
         connected over space)       connected over space)
                    \                 /
                     \               /
                  Environmental geography
                  (humans ↔ environment)
                           |
              Geospatial models & methods
              (maps, GIS, remote sensing…)
```

| Realm | What it asks | Example from lecture |
|---|---|---|
| **Physical** | How climate, soils, life, landforms connect | Banff: tree line, U-valley, glacier retreat |
| **Human** | How culture, economy, religion, politics connect in space | New Orleans vs Morocco cemeteries |
| **Environmental** | How people impact / depend on environment | Amazon road + deforestation (Brazil) |
| **Geospatial** | Tools to study the above | Maps, satellites, GIS, change-over-time photos |

## 3. Case studies（クイズで出やすい）

### A. Banff (Physical)
- Conifer forest → stops at **tree line** (cold, thin soils, long snow)
- Remnant **glacier** → climate change signal
- **U-shaped valley** = glaciation · **V-shaped** = river erosion
- Shows links among biosphere + atmosphere + lithosphere + hydrosphere

### B. Cemeteries (Human)
| | New Orleans | Morocco |
|---|---|---|
| Wealth | Elaborate, expensive graves | Modest neighborhood / graves |
| Religion | Crosses → Christianity | Simplicity valued → Muslim burial |
| Climate/site | Above-ground burial (high water table / sea level) | Drier climate (vegetation clue) |

### C. Brazil Amazon road (Environmental) — video still: Uruará, Pará
- Poor road → access unreliable → poverty / isolation clue
- Built for rapid extraction of forest resources
- **Reddish soils** = low organic matter, nutrient-poor → farming after clear-cut fails quickly

## 4. Tools
- Maps
- Satellite / aerial imagery (disaster response, fire mapping)
- Change over time (1937 farm/forest → 1971 suburb)
- **GIS**: stack layers (customers, roads, parcels, elevation, land use) → decisions (store site, turtle sanctuary)
