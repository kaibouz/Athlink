## Week 1 · Module 1 · The Geographic Grid System
**Video:** Lecture Video 2 · ~24 min  
**Slides:** 01GeogGrid.pdf  
**Due cluster:** Lab 1 + Quiz 1 · Mon Sep 14, 12:45 PM  

_スライドで *memorize* とある 7 parallels・23.5°・small vs large scale を優先暗記。_

### Objectives
- Identify basic components of the Geographic Grid
- Distinguish sphere vs oblate ellipsoid references
- Use latitude/longitude correctly
- Relate tilt (23.5°) to key parallels and seasons setup

### Key terms
| Term | English | 日本語 |
|---|---|---|
| Authalic sphere | Reference surface for small-scale maps | 小縮尺図の基準球 |
| Ellipsoid | Reference for detailed large-scale maps & GPS (Earth bulges at equator) | 大縮尺・GPS向け扁球 |
| Great circle | Divides Earth into equal halves (e.g., Equator) | 大円 |
| Small circle | Divides Earth unevenly (e.g., Arctic Circle) | 小円 |
| Graticule | Imaginary network of parallels + meridians | 経緯線網 |

### Notes

**A. Earth’s shape**
- Spherical model: Aristotle evidence; Eratosthenes ~250 BC circumference (~15% off)
- Gravity → oblate ellipsoid: bulge at equator, flatten at poles (Newton)
- Small-scale map (e.g. 1:1,000,000) → sphere OK; large-scale (e.g. 1:100) → ellipsoid/GPS

**B. Coordinates — memorize**
- Latitude = parallels E–W; 0° Equator → 90° N/S poles
- Longitude = meridians N–S; 0° Prime Meridian → 180° Date Line
- Axis tilt 23.5° off vertical → 7 key parallels (Tropics, Midlatitudes, Polar)
- Typical 7: Equator 0°; Tropic Cancer 23.5°N; Tropic Capricorn 23.5°S; Arctic 66.5°N; Antarctic 66.5°S; N Pole 90°N; S Pole 90°S
- Time zones: 24 zones around meridians 15° apart → 15° = 1 hour; UTC/GMT at 0°

**C. Geodetic position**
- Network of accurately measured ground points → mapping accuracy
- Longitude from time: (local − UTC hours) × 15° (needs chronometer)
- Example location style: 34°N, 118°W

## Week 2 · Module 2 · Earth–Sun Relations
**Video:** Lecture Video 1 of 2 · ~28 min

_Canvas Module 2 + 標準トピックで設営。視聴後に細部を追記予定。季節の理由・declination・solstice/equinox を中心に。_

### Objectives
- Demonstrate understanding of Earth–Sun relations and the seasons
- Explain why seasons happen (tilt + orbit, not mainly distance)

### Key terms
| Term | English | 日本語 |
|---|---|---|
| Rotation | Earth spins on its axis (~24 h) → day/night | 自転 |
| Revolution | Earth orbits the Sun (~365.25 d) | 公転 |
| Axial tilt | ~23.5° from perpendicular to orbital plane | 地軸の傾き |
| Declination (Sun) | Latitude of the subsolar point; migrates between 23.5°N–23.5°S | 太陽赤緯／直下点緯度 |
| Subsolar point | Where Sun is directly overhead (90° altitude) | 太陽直下点 |
| Circle of illumination | Boundary between day and night hemispheres | 明暗界線 |

### Notes

**Reasons for the seasons**
- Seasons ≠ mainly closer/farther from Sun
- Tilt keeps orientation roughly fixed as Earth revolves → changing day length & Sun angle
- June solstice: NH summer; Sun declination 23.5°N (Tropic of Cancer)
- December solstice: NH winter; declination 23.5°S (Tropic of Capricorn)
- Equinoxes (Mar/Sep): declination 0° (Equator); day/night ~equal globally

**Latitudinal zones (link to Module 1)**
- Tropics: between 23.5°N–23.5°S — can have overhead Sun
- Midlatitudes: between tropics and polar circles
- Polar: poleward of 66.5° — possible 24-h day/night seasons

## Week 3 · Module 3 · Insolation & Temperature Patterns
**Video:** Lecture Video 1 of 2 · ~28 min  
**Slides:** Insolation and Temperature Patterns PDF  
**Due cluster:** Lab 3 + Quiz 3 · Mon Sep 28, 12:45 PM  

_Module 3前半。insolation・熱の伝わり方・気温パターン。Khan Academy 電磁波クリップあり。_

### Objectives
- Analyze relationships among energy, temperature, and heat
- Describe how heat is transferred around the planet

### Key terms
| Term | English | 日本語 |
|---|---|---|
| Insolation | Incoming solar radiation received at Earth | 日射（入射太陽放射） |
| Albedo | Fraction of radiation reflected by a surface | アルベド（反射率） |
| Absorption | Energy taken in by atmosphere/surface → warms | 吸収 |
| Temperature | Measure of average kinetic energy of molecules | 温度 |
| Heat | Energy transfer due to temperature difference | 熱 |
| Continentality | Interiors heat/cool more than oceans (specific heat / mixing) | 大陸度 |

### Notes

**Energy & temperature patterns**
- Low latitudes receive more average insolation than poles → energy surplus tropics, deficit poles
- Atmosphere & oceans redistribute heat poleward
- Land heats/cools faster than water → coastal climates milder
- Elevation: temperature usually drops with height in troposphere

**Heat transfer modes**
- Radiation: electromagnetic waves (Sun → Earth; Earth emits IR)
- Conduction: molecule-to-molecule (surface ↔ air skin)
- Convection: vertical mixing of air/fluid
- Advection: horizontal transport of heat by wind/currents

## Week 3 · Module 3 · Air Pressure & Wind
**Video:** Lecture Video 2 of 2 · ~34 min  
**Slides:** Air Pressure and Wind PDF  
**Due cluster:** Lab 3 + Quiz 3 · Mon Sep 28, 12:45 PM  

_Module 3後半。気圧傾度・コリオリ・三細胞モデル。Khan Academy Global Wind Patterns 推奨。_

### Objectives
- Understand global patterns in air pressure
- Explain circulation of winds at planetary scale

### Key terms
| Term | English | 日本語 |
|---|---|---|
| Air pressure | Force of air per area; decreases with height | 気圧 |
| Pressure gradient | Change of pressure over distance; drives wind from high → low | 気圧傾度 |
| Coriolis effect | Apparent deflection due to rotation (right in NH, left in SH) | コリオリの力 |
| ITCZ | Intertropical Convergence Zone — equatorial low where trades meet | 熱帯収束帯 |
| Subtropical high | High-pressure belts ~30°; sinking air; many deserts | 亜熱帯高圧帯 |
| Trade winds | Surface winds from subtropical highs toward ITCZ | 貿易風 |

### Notes

**Forces on wind**
- Pressure gradient force: high → low
- Coriolis deflects moving air (NH right / SH left)
- Friction slows wind near surface and changes balance

**Three-cell model (idealized)**
- Hadley cell: tropics — rising near ITCZ, sinking ~30°
- Ferrel cell: midlatitudes (more complex / indirect)
- Polar cell: cold sinking near poles
- Surface: trades, westerlies, polar easterlies
