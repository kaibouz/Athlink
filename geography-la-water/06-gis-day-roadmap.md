## Demo script skeleton (2 minutes)

1. **Hook (15 sec):** *“LA’s tap is local. Its water is not. This map shows where vulnerability comes from.”*
2. **Sources (30 sec):** Click 2 sources · show ranges · say uncertainty out loud.
3. **Infrastructure (30 sec):** Open one dossier · distance · lift · energy.
4. **Scenario (30 sec):** Slide *Low Snowpack* · what changes on the map.
5. **Close (15 sec):** *“Illustrative model for public literacy — not an official forecast. QR for the live site.”*
